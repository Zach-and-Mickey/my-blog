**[2 Kings 17:41](https://biblehub.com/2_kings/17-41.htm)** *So these nations feared the LORD and also served their carved images. Their children did likewise, and their children’s children—as their fathers did, so they do to this day.*

I need to be cautious of this as well. I may not have any carved images, but I like to chase after my own desires and serve myself. I need to get to a point where I realize that serving the Lord is the only place I can find contentment in this life. He is the only one worthy of my service and attention. I spend way too much time thinking about other people and how I can please them without even considering what I need to be doing for God. And that brings me to another point. What do I need to be doing for God? I've been struggling lately with this because it feels like I'm not doing anything. Yeah I go to church and run the sound and help with recreation but where actually is God in all of that? It feels like such a routine at this point. Not really a good ending point but I'm gonna leave it at that.

>**[1 Samuel 7:3](https://biblehub.com/1_samuel/7-3.htm)** *Then Samuel said to all the house of Israel, “If you are returning to the LORD with all your hearts, then put away the foreign gods and Ashtoreths among you, prepare your hearts for the LORD, and serve Him only.*
