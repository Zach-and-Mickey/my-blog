**[2 Chronicles 12:14](https://biblehub.com/2_chronicles/12-14.htm)** *And he did evil, for he did not set his heart to seek the Lord.*

This passage is talking about Rehoboam. He becomes king of Judah after Solomon's death and kind of does okay but doesn't fully commit to following God. His heart was not set on seeking the Lord. I will never please God unless that is my true aim. What is my heart set on right now? Is it set on seeking God, or seeking the approval of man, or seeking comfort and control?

I would say that lately I haven't been hearing much from God. I feel kind of lost and like I need to be doing more but don't know what to do. I can't find the exact quote but I am reminded of a scene from *Prince Caspian*. Lucy sees Aslan as they are walking through the woods but no one else does. Peter asks why he wouln't have seen him, and Lucy's response is, "Maybe because you weren't looking." I had always thought that was just a way to include a little joke, but with Aslan being the Christ character, it seems much more likely that there is a deeper meaning. If you are seeking God you will find Him. Maybe I haven't been looking.

>**[Jeremiah 29:13](https://biblehub.com/jeremiah/29-13.htm)** *“You will seek Me and find Me when you search for Me with all your heart.*
